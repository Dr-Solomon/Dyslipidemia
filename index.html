Lipid-Lowering Therapies: Initial Dose, Escalation Rules & Max Dose Limits

1. Upfront Combination Therapy

Initiate statin + ezetimibe simultaneously when baseline LDL-C is too elevated for statin monotherapy to reach goal (e.g., Very High-Risk ASCVD with LDL-C >110 mg/dL or HeFH ≥190 mg/dL).

2. Rule of 6% vs Add-On Therapy

Doubling a statin dose provides only ~6% incremental LDL-C reduction while increasing myalgia risk. Adding Ezetimibe 10 mg adds ~20–25% lowering with superior tolerability (RACING trial).

3. 4–12 Week Lab Reassessment

Recheck lipid panel 4–12 weeks after initiation or dose titration to verify adherence and numeric LDL-C / Non-HDL-C target attainment, then every 3–12 months.

Special Clinical Situations & Contraindications

1. Pregnancy, Planned Conception & Lactation Class 3: Harm

Discontinue statins, ezetimibe, bempedoic acid, and PCSK9-directed therapies 1–2 months before planned conception, during pregnancy, and while breastfeeding. In severe FH requiring lipid lowering during pregnancy, non-absorbed bile acid sequestrants (colesevelam) or lipoprotein apheresis may be used.

2. Statin-Associated Muscle Symptoms (SAMS) Protocol

Evaluate CK, TSH, and vitamin D if severe myalgias or weakness develop. After 2–4 week washout, rechallenge with a lower dose, alternate-day rosuvastatin/atorvastatin, or pitavastatin. Combine moderate-intensity statin + ezetimibe 10 mg (RACING) or transition to bempedoic acid 180 mg + ezetimibe 10 mg FDC (CLEAR Outcomes) if completely statin-intolerant.

3. CKD Stage ≥3, HIV & Cardio-Oncology

CKD (Non-Dialysis): Initiate moderate-intensity statin ± ezetimibe (SHARP); prefer atorvastatin (no renal dose cap). Do not initiate statins de novo in hemodialysis unless already taking.
HIV (Age ≥40y): Pitavastatin 4 mg daily reduces MACE by 35% without antiretroviral CYP3A4 interactions (REPRIEVE).
Cardio-Oncology: Baseline lipid & ASCVD risk assessment prior to cardiotoxic or endocrine cancer therapies; manage dyslipidemia per standard 2026 risk tiers.

4. Older Adults (>75 Years)

Continue established statin therapy if well tolerated. In primary prevention >75y, consider initiating moderate-intensity statin after shared decision-making factoring in frailty, polypharmacy, life expectancy, and CAC score.
